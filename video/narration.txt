# 旁白文本 v2

[01-title.wav]
This is Arc Guardian. Spending guardrails, and a hash-chained decision ledger, for AI agents that pay in USDC on Arc.

[02-problem.wav]
Give an agent a wallet and it will pay. The problem is what it pays: an invoice that gets sent twice, a run that crashes and retries, a new supplier that is not on your vendor list. And a language model will always find a reason. Circle's wallet policies exist on mainnet. On testnet there are none, and an address cap does not know what an invoice is.

[03-architecture.wav]
So money leaves through one door. The agent proposes a payment. A deterministic policy engine, written by the owner and never editable by the model, checks it: known vendor, spending caps, duplicates, risk tier, approval threshold. An allow goes to the Circle agent wallet and settles on Arc. A hold waits for a person. A deny stops the payment. Every step is appended to a hash-chained ledger.

[04-invoices.wav]
Six real invoices from my one-person shop. Three are ChatGPT, billed in Indonesian rupiah. One is Webshare. Two are proxy vendors. A sample accounts-payable agent reads them with Gemini, judges each one, and writes down why.

[05-ap-run.wav]
Webshare: already paid, amount due is zero, so skip. The July ChatGPT invoice: the registry lists this vendor in USDC, the invoice is in rupiah, and the owner's note about the plan change does not cover July, so hold for a person. August: the amount decreased because of a verified plan change from Plus to Go, as noted in the registry, so pay. Each payable invoice is converted at the day's rate into USDC, checked against the treasury balance, oldest first, and then queued for payment.

[06-guardian.wav]
Three payments pass Guardian and settle on Arc testnet, gas paid in USDC. Now the vendor sends the August invoice again. Same invoice number. The model says pay. Guardian denies it: this invoice is already settled. Then September arrives. It is a new invoice, but the same amount as the one just paid, so Guardian holds it for a person. The model cannot talk past either rule.

[07-approval.wav]
A person approves, in one command. The approval is written to the ledger and bound to the exact recipient, amount and currency. Change the amount and the token is void. The agent resubmits, Guardian allows, and it settles.

[08-explorer.wav]
Here it is on the Arc explorer. A Circle agent wallet, ERC-4337. Four point one eight USDC to the vendor. The fee is a fraction of a cent, paid in USDC. Arc confirmed it in under half a second.

[09-ledger.wav]
Everything the agent saw and concluded is on a public page: the reasoning, which rules fired, every transaction. The page just recomputed every hash in the browser: twenty-one entries, chain intact. Edit one line after the fact, and it breaks at that entry.

[10-close.wav]
Traction so far: one business, mine. Four invoices settled, twenty-three dollars in USDC, on Arc testnet. Guardian ships as a library, a CLI, and a worker that watches a folder, so any agent can use it. Arc Guardian. Give the agent the keys — behind a door it cannot talk its way through.
